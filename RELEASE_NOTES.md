**Download:** `Reactor-Studio-Mac-arm64.dmg` for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer), or `Reactor-Studio-Mac-x64.dmg` for older Intel Macs.

Open the .dmg and drag **Reactor Studio** into Applications.

First time only: if macOS says it can't verify the app, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
